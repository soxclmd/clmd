How to install

  1. Download the APKTap the button above or scan the QR code.
  2. Allow app installsWhen asked, tap Settings → Allow from this source, then go back. (Android shows this for any app outside the Play Store.)
  3. InstallTap Install. You may see "Unknown developer" — expected, since DepEd RO XII signed it directly.
  4. Open CLMD BulletinLaunch the app from your home screen. Done!
Heads-up: Android warns about apps not from the Play Store. This APK is signed with the CLMD Region XII certificate and only loads the official portal at soxclmd.github.io/clmd — it requests just internet permission and collects nothing.

How updates work

The app loads the live CLMD Bulletin website every time it opens. Every memo, advisory, or event you post to the site appears in the app automatically — no app update needed. New APK versions are only needed if the website address changes.
