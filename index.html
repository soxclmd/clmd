Region XII Validates Community Heritage Learning Centers in Kidapawan City

DepEd Region XII conducted the Regional Validation of Community Heritage Learning Centers (CHLC) in SDO Kidapawan on September 22–23, 2026, assessing the readiness, functionality, and cultural relevance of identified sites in Nungauan, Ginantilan, and Balabag as community-based spaces for preserving and transmitting Indigenous Peoples’ knowledge and heritage.
